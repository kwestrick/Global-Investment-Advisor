Risk Dashboard

COP/USD Volatility (HIGH)
13.7% annualized. COP can depreciate sharply. A 10% COP depreciation erases ~4pp of real yield for a USD investor. Keep COP position sized to risk tolerance.
Colombia Political Risk (HIGH)
Petro administration policies may affect TES issuance, Ecopetrol dividends, and capital flows. Monitor fiscal deficit trajectory and Banrep independence.
TES Duration Risk (MEDIUM)
Long-duration TES (10Y, 30Y) are rate-sensitive. Banrep cutting cycle favors capital gains; inflation resurgence would cause losses. Favor 5Y to limit duration exposure.
U.S. Equity Drawdown (MEDIUM)
At 60-70% equity target, expect 25-35% drawdowns in bear markets. Guaranteed income ($7,488/month) provides a floor. Do not sell equities in downturns.
Excess USD Cash Drag (MEDIUM)
$309k in USD liquid cash is 135 months of expenses. Deploying Phase 1 ($210k per target allocation) is the biggest single near-term wealth-building action.
CDT Credit Risk (LOW)

Ecopetrol Oil Price Risk (LOW-MED)
