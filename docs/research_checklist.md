# Research Checklist

Use this checklist before treating any investment thesis as complete.

## Setup

- [ ] Research question is clearly stated.
- [ ] Time horizon is defined.
- [ ] U.S. benchmark is defined.
- [ ] Investable vehicle is identified.
- [ ] Data sources are documented.

## Market Evidence

- [ ] Total return is compared against the benchmark.
- [ ] Volatility is reviewed.
- [ ] Drawdown risk is reviewed.
- [ ] Relative strength is reviewed.
- [ ] Currency-adjusted performance is considered where relevant.

## Valuation Evidence

- [ ] Current valuation is reviewed.
- [ ] Valuation is compared with history.
- [ ] Valuation is compared with U.S. alternatives.
- [ ] Valuation is compared with regional or sector peers.
- [ ] Dividend yield, earnings yield, or real yield is considered where relevant.

## Macro Evidence

- [ ] Growth trend is reviewed.
- [ ] Inflation trend is reviewed.
- [ ] Central bank policy is reviewed.
- [ ] Fiscal position is reviewed.
- [ ] Current-account position is reviewed.
- [ ] Currency vulnerability is reviewed.

## Geopolitical and Policy Evidence

- [ ] Political stability is reviewed.
- [ ] Sanctions or capital-control risk is reviewed.
- [ ] Conflict exposure is reviewed.
- [ ] Policy credibility is reviewed.
- [ ] Energy security or commodity dependence is reviewed if relevant.

## Thesis Quality

- [ ] Bull case is stated.
- [ ] Bear case is stated.
- [ ] Catalysts are identified.
- [ ] Falsification triggers are identified.
- [ ] Liquidity and implementation risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Evidence gaps are acknowledged.

## Final Output

- [ ] Conclusion is conditional, not certain.
- [ ] The case for outperformance versus the U.S. is explicit.
- [ ] The strongest reason to avoid the opportunity is stated.
- [ ] The required disclaimer is included.
